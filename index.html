Questions

Waste Removal FAQ

What type of waste do you remove?

We accept enquiries for general household waste, garden waste, building rubble, bulk waste, recyclable materials and other suitable waste streams.

Do you remove garden waste?

Yes. Garden waste removal can include suitable grass, leaves, branches and tree cuttings.

Do you remove building rubble?

We accept enquiries for suitable non-hazardous building and renovation rubble. Send a photo and details so the job can be assessed.

Can I send a photo of my waste?

Yes. WhatsApp is a convenient way to send photographs of the waste so we can better understand the job before discussing the quotation.

Do you offer skip bins?

You can contact us with your skip-bin requirements, including the type of waste, approximate volume, location and preferred dates.

Do you collect hazardous waste?

Hazardous waste requires specialist handling and appropriate disposal arrangements. Contact us first with details of the material so that the correct service route can be determined.
