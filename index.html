Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before requesting collection.

How do I get a quote?

Send us your location, type of waste and approximate amount. Photos can also be sent through WhatsApp.

Do you collect garden waste?

Yes. We can arrange removal of garden waste such as branches, leaves, grass and other garden clean-up waste.

Do you remove building rubble?

Yes, building and renovation rubble can be quoted depending on the amount and access to the site.

Can you remove furniture?

Yes. Contact us with details of the furniture and collection location so we can provide a quote.

Do you collect from businesses?

Yes. We can discuss waste removal requirements for businesses, offices and other commercial properties.

Can I send photos on WhatsApp?

Yes. Photos are useful because they can help us understand the amount and type of waste before quoting.
